# 04: Creative Assets

## Images (ChatGPT Go + Nano Banana)

**Build approach:** the site ships with **placeholders** for every image below. You generate images whenever ready and upload them from the admin panel (Content & media). No redeploy needed.
Tip: Nano Banana is best at keeping **your face consistent** from 2 to 3 reference selfies. Use ChatGPT for layout-style graphics and text-heavy banners. Always add the text in the website, not baked in the image, so we can edit it and keep images light.

| # | Asset | Size | Tool | Where used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero portrait of you (confident, laptop, warm studio light) | 1200×1500 (mobile), 1920×1080 (desktop) | Nano Banana | Hero |
| 2 | You on stage / presenting webinar | 1200×800 | Nano Banana | About / bonus |
| 3 | "System" diagram background (Idea → Create → Automate → Sell) icons | 1600×600 | ChatGPT | Section 6 |
| 4 | Laptop + phone mockup showing sales notifications | 1200×900 | ChatGPT | Proof |
| 5 | Ad creatives: square 1080×1080, vertical 1080×1920 | Both | Nano Banana | Meta ads |
| 6 | Open Graph share image | 1200×630 | ChatGPT | WhatsApp/Facebook link previews |
| 7 | Favicon / logo "GenZy Club" | 512×512 | ChatGPT | Site |

**Never fake the dashboard image.** Use your real Razorpay screenshot for proof (blur customer details). Fake income screenshots get ads rejected and hurt trust.

### Prompt template (Nano Banana, with your selfies attached)
> "Use the person in the reference photos. Photorealistic portrait of this person sitting at a modern desk with a laptop showing a sales dashboard, warm cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, dark background with subtle purple and orange accents, confident smile, looking at camera, empty space on the left for text, 4:5 vertical."

### Brand look (proposal)
- Colours: dark background (#0B0B12), accent orange (#FF6B2C), success green (#22C55E), white text
- Font: Poppins or Inter (bold headlines)
- Vibe: Gen-Z, energetic, premium but not corporate

## Ad videos (Google Flow)
Make 3 to 4 short vertical videos (9:16, 15 to 30 sec) to test which hook wins. Each one: **hook in first 2 seconds → proof → what you'll learn → CTA**.

| Video | Hook (first 2 sec, Hinglish) |
|---|---|
| A: Proof hook | "1 hafte mein ₹1,00,000. Ek bhi physical product nahi." |
| B: Curiosity | "Main so raha tha aur mere phone pe payments aa rahe the." |
| C: Pain | "Job ki salary kam pad rahi hai? Yeh digital system try karo." |
| D: You talking | Real selfie video of you (30 sec) — often beats AI video in India. Strongly recommended as one of the tests. |

End card for all: "LIVE Webinar · 7 to 10 Nov · ₹499 · Link in bio / Book Now".

Note: AI video of a real person must be labelled as AI-generated where Meta requires it.
